About

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. candidate in Bioinformatics at Ewha Womans University, Seoul, South Korea, advised by Prof. Wankyu Kim in the Systems Pharmacology Laboratory.

My research focuses on AI-driven drug and target discovery. I use transcriptomic shifts induced by genetic or pharmacological perturbations to identify potential therapeutic targets with AI. What interests me most is building foundation models that make in silico perturbation experiments genuinely useful for drug discovery.

scDEBART: Predicting in silico Single-Cell Perturbation Responses via Large-Scale Differential Expression Learning

A foundation model for single-cell perturbation response, pretrained to predict log fold-changes rather than absolute expression.

TDEP: Predicting Target Perturbation Response from Drug-Induced Transcriptomes

A deep neural network that predicts transcriptome-wide responses to target inhibition, combining pretrained protein language model embeddings with graph attention propagation over a co-expression network under drug–target interaction supervision.

  • L1000 Connectivity Map
  • AUROC 0.873 unseen drug

FateDriver: A Dual-View Graph Neural Network for Predicting Driver Genes in Cellular Reprogramming

Learns from the reprogramming factors a protocol delivers, then returns the endogenous genes that align the same way.

Sequence-Similarity–Guided Multi-Representation Ensemble for MAP3K5 IC50 Prediction
