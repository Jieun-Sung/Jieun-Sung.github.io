scDEBART

A BART encoder–decoder foundation model for single-cell perturbation response. It reframes the pretraining objective from reconstructing absolute expression to predicting log fold-changes conditioned on basal expression, which lets it generalize inductively to perturbations it has never seen.

  • 6.28M DE profiles / 66.6M cells
  • 4–7× top-gene recovery
  • 71.4% top-1 reverse perturbation
